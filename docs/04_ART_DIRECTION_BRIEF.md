# KNLSOFT Art Direction Brief v0.1

## Concept
**Proven Database Expertise. Evolved with Intelligence.**

The website must look like an established enterprise DB/SQL technology company whose expertise is advancing through responsible AI.

## Visual character
Established, precise, intelligent, reliable, engineered, enterprise-ready.

## Tone
- Bright, editorial base canvas
- Controlled dark-navy contrast sections
- Product-specific accent colors
- Strong imagery and typographic hierarchy
- Evidence-rich rather than decoration-rich
- Dark surfaces limited to roughly 25–35%

## Visual roles
- Corporate: navy, white, disciplined blue
- aTops: quality/safety-oriented green or teal, pending BI confirmation
- SPACEMON: observation/performance-oriented blue or violet, pending BI confirmation
- AI: a restrained analytical accent, never rainbow or neon

## Hero contract
Within five seconds the visitor must understand:
1. KNLSOFT specializes in DB and SQL.
2. aTops and SPACEMON are real core products.
3. AI strengthens existing expertise.

The hero must not use a generic left-copy/right-dashboard template, obsolete full UI, robot/brain/circuit imagery, meaningless data lines, fabricated metrics, or an improvised logo.

## Image strategy
- 30% brand and technology photography
- 25% carefully processed real product UI
- 20% technical illustration
- 15% customer and deployment evidence
- 10% icons and supporting graphics

## Product UI levels
1. Real UI: details, guides, demonstrations
2. Processed real UI: homepage product sections
3. Clearly labeled concept visualization: hero or AI explanation

## AI visual language
Raw data → rule/statistical analysis → AI interpretation → evidence and recommendation → expert decision.

## Layout
1440px maximum frame, 1240–1320px content grid, 12 desktop / 8 tablet / 4 mobile columns. Avoid repeated cards and centered text sections.

## Motion
Use motion only to clarify analysis, comparison, focus, or navigation. No neon pulse, perpetual data flow, 3D camera movement, or reading-blocking transitions.

## Approval gate
The design must be distinctive, financially credible, evidence-based, implementable with real assets, mobile-expandable, and free of template/PPT aesthetics.
