# KNLSoft Hero Visual Asset Plan v0.1

## 1. Hero objective

The first viewport must establish KNLSOFT as an experienced enterprise DB/SQL technology company, introduce aTops and SPACEMON as real products, and position AI as an evolution of proven expertise.

### Five-second comprehension test
A new visitor must understand:
1. KNLSOFT specializes in database and SQL technology.
2. aTops manages SQL quality before production.
3. SPACEMON manages database performance during operation.
4. AI supports analysis and expert judgment.

## 2. Message hierarchy

### Recommended primary headline
> SQL 품질부터 데이터베이스 성능까지

### Supporting statement
> 케이엔엘소프트는 aTops와 SPACEMON을 통해 개발 단계의 SQL 품질과 운영 단계의 데이터베이스 성능을 관리합니다.

### AI statement
> 축적된 DB·SQL 전문성에 AI 분석을 더합니다.

The AI statement must remain secondary until production AI evidence is verified.

### CTA
- Primary: 제품 살펴보기
- Secondary: 데모·도입 상담

Do not place brochure download, support, news, or multiple conversion actions in the Hero.

## 3. Visual protagonist

The Hero's visual protagonist is not a dashboard, robot, server rack, or abstract data tunnel. It is a carefully art-directed enterprise technology scene expressing:

- a real expert interpreting DB/SQL evidence
- operational scale and responsibility
- the transition from pre-production quality to live operations
- credible human judgment supported by technology

### Preferred construction
A layered editorial composition using:
1. commissioned or properly licensed enterprise technology photography
2. restrained processed fragments of real aTops and SPACEMON evidence
3. a custom DB/SQL analytical motif
4. real web typography rendered in HTML, never embedded in generated imagery

## 4. Composition contract

### Desktop 1440px
- Header: 76–84px
- Content grid: 12 columns, 1240–1320px
- Copy: columns 1–6
- Visual scene: columns 6–12 with controlled overlap
- Trust strip: visible at or immediately below fold
- Product names: compact role labels, not large UI cards

### Hero content limit
- one headline
- one supporting paragraph
- one AI line
- two CTAs
- two product role labels
- one trust/evidence strip

### Visual density
- high visual richness
- low textual density
- one focal image
- no collection of unrelated floating cards
- no empty expanses filled only by gradients or lines

## 5. Product visibility

### aTops
Display as:
> 운영 전 SQL 품질·위험 관리

Permitted visual fragments:
- SQL before/after comparison
- quality rule or risk evidence
- analysis result excerpt
- review or approval state

### SPACEMON
Display as:
> 운영 중 DB 성능·이상 관리

Permitted visual fragments:
- temporal performance change
- DB/SQL/session relationship
- anomaly context
- narrowed cause candidate

### Restrictions
- no complete obsolete UI in Hero
- no stacked browser-window collage
- no fabricated modernized screen represented as real
- no implication of a single unified console without evidence
- no equal-size product cards that make the Hero look like a product selector

## 6. AI representation

AI must be shown as a transformation of evidence, not a decorative object.

### Visual sequence
Raw operational evidence → detected relationship → interpretable insight → expert decision

### Permitted elements
- highlighted causal relationship
- evidence-backed recommendation
- confidence or verification marker when actually supported
- human review indicator
- clear separation of rule/statistical and AI analysis

### Prohibited
- robot, brain, circuit face, glowing orb
- autonomous approval or remediation
- 100% accuracy implication
- unverified natural-language chat
- rainbow AI gradient
- generic AI sparkle icon as the main subject

## 7. Photography plan

### Required Hero shoot
Produce one primary scene and three alternates.

#### Scene A — primary
A senior DB/SQL expert in a refined enterprise workspace, reviewing a large but realistic performance-analysis surface. Camera angle emphasizes expertise and operational responsibility, not the person's face.

#### Scene B
Two specialists reviewing SQL quality and database performance evidence together, expressing development-to-operations continuity.

#### Scene C
A controlled operations environment with human presence and visible analytical work, avoiding futuristic NOC clichés.

#### Scene D
Macro editorial details: hands, annotated analysis, code, operational charts, and physical workspace texture for responsive crops and supporting sections.

### Photography rules
- realistic Korean enterprise environment
- neutral white, gray, graphite, and controlled brand color
- natural or architectural lighting
- no forced smiles or staged handshake
- no intense blue lighting
- no fabricated company office represented as factual
- secure rights for web, proposal, and social reuse

## 8. Product UI asset plan

### Required current captures
aTops:
1. development/production SQL comparison
2. SQL quality diagnosis
3. performance diagnosis
4. review/history state
5. AI analysis result, if production-ready

SPACEMON:
1. integrated DB view
2. SQL analysis
3. session/wait/lock analysis
4. anomaly or alert flow
5. AI analysis result, if production-ready

### Capture specification
- current supported release
- 2x desktop resolution minimum
- consistent 16:10 source viewport
- customer and system data masked
- no browser or operating-system chrome unless required
- version, source owner, capture date, and public-use status recorded

### Processing
- crop to evidence, not decoration
- preserve real hierarchy and values
- add website annotation as a separate layer
- never alter function names or invent results
- retain untouched originals alongside processed derivatives

## 9. Trust evidence strip

Place immediately after the Hero unless approved evidence is strong enough for an in-Hero strip.

### Candidate evidence
- approved financial/public customer logos
- verified supported DBMS
- official certifications and patents
- measured customer outcome
- years of DB specialization, only if officially verified
- On-Premise or closed-network support, only if product-specific evidence exists

### Minimum launch requirement
At least one verified item from three of these categories:
1. customer or case
2. technology support
3. corporate credential
4. measured outcome

Do not use placeholder logos or invented figures.

## 10. Motion plan

### Allowed
- subtle image depth on entry
- sequential reveal of aTops and SPACEMON role labels
- precise emphasis on a real UI evidence fragment
- restrained transition from pre-operation to operation
- CTA and navigation micro-interactions

### Timing
- entry sequence: 600–900ms
- individual transitions: 180–320ms
- no motion required to understand the message
- reduced-motion alternative mandatory

### Prohibited
- perpetual data-line animation
- pulsing neon
- rotating 3D object
- autoplay dashboard simulation
- long cinematic intro
- motion that delays navigation or text

1. company and product message
2. primary visual crop
3. product role labels
4. primary CTA

- performance target requires responsive images and no blocking video

## 14. Approval harness

A Hero candidate passes only when all conditions are satisfied.

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Identity | Recognizable as a DB/SQL enterprise company without relying on logo |
| Clarity | Company and two product roles understood within five seconds |
| Trust | Looks established, financially credible, and operationally responsible |
| Truthfulness | Every feature, screen, logo, and number has approved evidence |
| Visual finish | Does not resemble a template, slide, dashboard collage, or concept art |
| Density | Rich but not crowded; no empty artificial space |
| Product balance | aTops and SPACEMON are visible without competing with company message |
| AI restraint | AI supports expertise and does not become an unsupported promise |
| Responsive | Desktop and mobile compositions both work |
| Accessibility | Readability, contrast, focus, motion, and alternative text pass |
| Performance | Hero assets meet agreed loading targets |
| Reusability | Source, derivatives, and rights are registered |

## 15. Failure routing

- Missing official logo → block brand-final approval; continue composition with a clearly marked placeholder only in internal review.
- Missing current product UI → do not substitute old UI; continue with photography and approved product labels.
- Missing AI evidence → remove AI visual claim and retain only verified roadmap wording.
- Missing customer approval → use supported-technology evidence instead.
- Low-quality photography → do not compensate with gradients or abstract lines; reshoot or license a better asset.
- Candidate looks like a slide → reject and rebuild the composition before web implementation.
