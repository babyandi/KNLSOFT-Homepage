# KNLSOFT Design Reference Benchmark v0.1

## Reference synthesis
| Reference | Adopt | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Dynatrace | Strong positioning, AI→platform→customer proof flow | Autonomous-AI claims without evidence |
| Datadog | Product/technology/use-case information architecture | Oversized mega-menu and SaaS trial logic |
| Elastic | Evolution from proven core technology into AI | Lightweight developer-brand tone |
| IBM watsonx | Enterprise AI governance, security, restraint | Abstract, oversized AI narrative |
| Splunk | Customer problem and operational-resilience framing | Expanding scope into unsupported categories |
| Snowflake | Editorial layouts, brand consistency, customer proof | Cloud-universe imagery and excessive 3D |

## Recommended combination
Elastic's expertise-evolution structure + IBM's enterprise trust + Dynatrace's persuasion flow + Snowflake's visual finish.

## KNLSoft application rules
1. Lead with DB/SQL expertise, not AI fashion.
2. Put verified trust evidence near the top.
3. Separate aTops and SPACEMON product roles.
4. Explain AI together with security, evidence, and expert approval.
5. Use varied editorial scenes instead of repeated cards.
6. Optimize conversion for consultation and demos, not free SaaS trials.
7. Never imitate the scale or unsupported platform claims of global vendors.
